    The person behind The Zivraj

    I’m Jibraj, the creator of The Zivraj, a YouTube channel for young people in Nepal who are preparing for government service. I started it to make recruitment information easier to find and easier to understand.

    On the channel I cover Nepal Police, Nepal Army, Armed Police Force and Lok Sewa preparation, along with job updates and career guidance. My viewers are students and aspirants, so I try to keep every video clear, practical and honest.

    Outside of the channel, I design graphics and enjoy exploring new technology and digital tools. This site is where all of that comes together.
